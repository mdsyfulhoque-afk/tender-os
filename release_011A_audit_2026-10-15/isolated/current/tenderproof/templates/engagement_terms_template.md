# TenderProof Rapid Bid Diagnostic - Engagement Terms

Scope:
- Review ToR/source documents supplied by the client.
- Normalize requirements into a compliance and proposal-readiness structure.
- Identify gaps, CV risks, bid/no-bid considerations, and clarification questions.
- Prepare diagnostic report and Excel workbook.

Limitations:
- This diagnostic does not guarantee tender success.
- Evidence gaps remain the client's responsibility unless separately contracted.
- AI-assisted outputs require human review before submission.

Delivery target:
- 48-72 hours after all required inputs are received.
