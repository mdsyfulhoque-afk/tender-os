- ToR extraction into atomic requirements

- Bid/no-bid scorecard
- Gap list and risk register
- Clarification questions
- Proposal skeleton
- Methodology outline
- Diagnostic report and Excel workbook

Best fit:
- Consulting firms
- NGOs
- Research organizations
- Independent experts preparing donor-funded proposals
