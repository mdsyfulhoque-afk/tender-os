# Bangladesh e-GP Operating Notes

Reviewed 2026-10-12. Internal product guidance, not legal advice or an official BPPA checklist.

## Source hierarchy

1. Check the current Act/Rules, gazettes, circulars, transition provisions, tender notice/document and portal amendments for each bid.
2. BPPA states PPR 2025 took effect on 28 September 2025 and comprises 154 Rules and 21 Schedules. BPPA also published a PPA amendment in 2026 and a clarification circular about PPR 2025 Rule 154; verify the current documents rather than inferring legal effect from a news headline.
3. The official e-GP Guidelines (Revised) 2025 explain electronic operations, but their text refers to PPR 2008 in places. Do not use that operational guideline alone to determine the governing law.
4. The user-supplied 144-scan guide is a mixed Bangla/English operating guide with scanned pages and an appended e-GP Guidelines (Revised) 2025 section. Its page-indexed OCR is in `data/reference/e_gp_tender_book_ocr.txt`; OCR may misread Bangla, tables, numbers, and forms. Verify every statement against the image and current official publications.

## Product behavior

- Keep e-GP case readiness separate from donor ToR compliance and the official portal's own eligibility/evaluation.
- Never assume a universal tender-security amount, signature type, document list, or procurement-rule transition. Ask the user to verify the exact tender data sheet and current portal instructions.
- Track sources, versions, dates, amendment status, evidence notes, and human review.
- A pre-award diagnostic must not imply that a tender has been submitted. The bidder must submit through e-GP and retain the portal receipt.
- Avoid storing identity-document numbers or credentials in this local checklist.
- Treat e-GP/SLM/NLP analysis as decision support. Preserve page/section references, identify OCR uncertainty, and require human confirmation.
- Use deterministic NLP for normalization, dates, identifiers, checklist completion, and consistency checks. An SLM is a possible language-analysis component within NLP, not an alternative to NLP; any future SLM should return source-grounded structured claims, uncertainty and page anchors for human review.

## Workflow areas covered

- Account type and registration evidence.
- Opportunity reference, Bangladesh Standard Time deadline, current document, and amendments.
- Eligibility, qualification, forms, schedules, reusable library evidence, and price/BOQ reconciliation.
- Tender-specific security and authorized signature checks.
- Upload review, on-time e-lodgment, and portal confirmation.
- Notification of Award, contract/security steps, and e-CMS work plans/milestones where applicable.
